(Integration notes)
- Cafe dishes endpoints validate dish payloads, support photo_url (data URLs or http(s)), and archive on delete.
- POS integration points:
  - `app/api/payments/route.js` and `app/api/payment-history/route.js` record receipts/payments and update notifications.
  - `app/api/audit-logs/cashier-sales` attempts to reconcile POS receipts with reservations and events.
- If `pos.receipts` table exists in the DB, several endpoints prefer it over `receipts`.

Files: see `app/api/cafe_dishes/*` and `app/api/dishes/route.js`.

-- Notifications & housekeeping (POS connection)

- When a POS transaction affects a reservation (for example, full payment), the payments path will compute totals and may insert a housekeeping notification so on-site staff can prepare the room. See `app/api/payments/route.js` for the insertion into `notifications`.

- The POS should always call `POST /api/payment-history` to persist receipt metadata; this table is used by cashier reports and by `audit-logs` reconciliation.

- For frontdesk / cashier UIs: after saving to `payment_history` the UI should also consider calling `POST /api/payments` when the payment affects reservation balances so housekeeping notifications and reservation totals are updated atomically.

- Notifications consumption:
  - Housekeeping clients fetch `GET /api/notifications` filtered by `target_role='Housekeeping'`.
  - The service worker at `public/sw.js` is present to show push notifications if push delivery is configured; otherwise clients poll `GET /api/notifications` or use a websocket/polling layer.

-- Audit & reporting

- POS receipts are surfaced to `app/api/audit-logs/cashier-sales` and `app/api/payment-history` and are the primary data sources for cashier sales reports. If you need a reproducible script to replay receipts for reconciliation, I can add one under `scripts/`.
