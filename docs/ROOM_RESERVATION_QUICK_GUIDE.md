# Room Reservation Quick Guide

## For Front Desk Staff

### Creating a Walk-in Reservation (Quick Process)

1. **Click "Walk-in Reservation"** (blue button)
   - Check-in date auto-fills to today
   - Walk-in timestamp added to remarks

2. **Enter Customer Details**
   - Name, Email, Phone (required)
   - Address, Nationality (optional)
   - Select Check-out date

3. **Select Package**
   - Choose room package from dropdown
   - View pricing and details

4. **Upload Customer ID**
   - File upload OR camera capture
   - Must be valid government ID

5. **Choose Payment Option**
   
   **Option A: Full Payment at Checkout**
   - Customer pays entire amount when leaving
   - No payment now
   
   **Option B: Downpayment Now**
   - Enter amount customer is paying now
   - Tip: Usually 50% of total
   - Rest due at checkout

6. **Review Payment Summary**
   - Check total amount
   - Verify downpayment (if applicable)
   - Confirm balance due

7. **Click "Create Reservation"**
   - System assigns room automatically
   - Prints contract and bill
   - Give copy to customer

---

## For Regular Reservations (Future Dates)

1. **Click "Add Reservation"** (green button)
2. **Follow same steps as walk-in** but:
   - Select future check-in date
   - Check-out date must be after check-in
3. **Payment options same as walk-in**

---

## Payment Options Explained

### Full Payment at Checkout ✅
- **When to use**: Customer prefers to pay at end of stay
- **What it means**: No payment required now
- **Contract shows**: "Payment Due at Checkout"
- **Amount due**: Full total when checking out

### Downpayment Now 💰
- **When to use**: Customer wants to secure reservation with deposit
- **What it means**: Partial payment now, rest later
- **Recommended amount**: 50% of total price
- **Contract shows**: 
  - Downpayment amount paid
  - Remaining balance due at checkout

---

## Contract Printing

After creating reservation:
1. System asks: "Would you like to print the contract and bill?"
2. Click "Print Contract & Bill"
3. New window opens with contract
4. Automatically prints
5. Give printed copy to customer
6. Customer should sign and return one copy

**Contract includes:**
- Confirmation number
- Guest information
- Room number assigned
- Package details
- Payment information
- Terms and conditions
- Signature lines

---

## Tips for Success

✅ **DO:**
- Verify customer ID matches name on reservation
- Explain payment terms clearly to customer
- Give customer printed contract copy
- Confirm email address for future communication
- Ask about special requests or requirements

❌ **DON'T:**
- Accept downpayment higher than total amount
- Create reservation without valid ID
- Skip contract printing (important for records)
- Forget to explain balance due at checkout

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Walk-in Guest, Full Payment Later
- Use "Walk-in Reservation"
- Select "Full Payment at Checkout"
- No money collected now
- Guest pays full amount when leaving

### Scenario 2: Walk-in Guest, Pays Deposit
- Use "Walk-in Reservation"
- Select "Downpayment Now"
- Enter amount customer paying (e.g., ₱2,000)
- Collect payment and give receipt
- Note balance due on contract

### Scenario 3: Future Reservation with Deposit
- Use "Add Reservation"
- Enter future dates
- Select "Downpayment Now"
- Collect deposit to secure booking
- Customer pays balance at check-in/checkout

### Scenario 4: Future Reservation, Pay Later
- Use "Add Reservation"
- Enter future dates
- Select "Full Payment at Checkout"
- No payment needed
- Customer pays when they arrive/leave

## Troubleshooting

**Problem**: Can't capture ID photo
- **Solution**: Check camera permissions in browser settings

**Problem**: Downpayment amount shows error
- **Solution**: Make sure amount is positive and not more than total

**Problem**: No rooms available
- **Solution**: Check if rooms exist for selected package in Room Status tab

**Problem**: Can't select past dates
- **Solution**: System only allows today or future dates (intentional)

**Problem**: Contract won't print
- **Solution**: Allow popups in browser settings
