# Quick Start Guide: Reservation Approval System

## For Managers

### Accessing the System
1. Log in to the dashboard
2. Navigate to **Reservation Management** in the sidebar
3. Click **Reservation Approval**

### Approving a Reservation

#### Step 1: View Pending Reservations
- Reservations appear under the "Pending" tab
- Each card shows:
  - Customer name and contact
  - Reservation type (ROOM or EVENT)
  - Total amount
  - Submission date

#### Step 2: Review Details
- Click the eye icon 👁️ to view full details
- Review customer information, dates, and special requests

#### Step 3: Approve or Reject
**To Approve:**
1. Click the green "Approve" button ✅
2. Select Payment Option:
   - **Pay Upon Checkout**: Customer pays everything at checkout
   - **Require Downpayment**: Customer pays partial amount now
3. If downpayment selected:
   - Enter downpayment amount (default: 30% of total)
   - System shows remaining balance automatically
4. Enter your manager credentials:
   - Email
   - Password
5. Click "Confirm Approval"

**To Reject:**
1. Click the red "Reject" button ❌
2. Enter rejection reason
3. Enter your manager credentials
4. Confirm rejection

### Managing Downpayments

#### Confirming Downpayment Receipt
1. Go to "Awaiting Downpayment" tab
2. Find the reservation
3. Click "Confirm Downpayment Received" 💰
4. Enter your manager credentials
5. Confirm

The system will:
- Move reservation to "Downpayment Paid" status
- Create entry in reservation history
- Make contract available for printing

### Printing Contracts

#### When to Print:
- After approval (with or without downpayment)
- After downpayment confirmation
- Upon customer request
- At checkout for final receipt

#### How to Print:
1. Click "Print Contract" button 🖨️
2. Review the contract on screen
3. Click "Print" in the modal
4. Browser print dialog opens
5. Select printer and print

#### Contract Includes:
- Hotel logo and invoice number
- Customer information
- Reservation details (dates, room/event, guests)
- Payment breakdown (total, downpayment, balance)
- Terms & Conditions
- Signature blocks for customer and staff

### Understanding Status Badges

| Status | Color | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Pending Approval | Yellow | Awaiting your approval |
| Awaiting Downpayment | Blue | Approved, waiting for customer payment |
| Downpayment Received | Green | Customer paid downpayment |
| Approved | Emerald | Fully approved (no downpayment) |
| Rejected | Red | Reservation rejected |
| Completed | Gray | Checkout completed |

### Using Tabs Efficiently

- **Pending**: New reservations needing approval
- **Awaiting Downpayment**: Approved, waiting for payment
- **Downpayment Paid**: Payment received, ready for customer
- **Approved**: Fully approved reservations
- **Rejected**: Declined reservations (for reference)
- **All**: View everything in one place

## For Front Desk Staff

### At Check-in (After Approval)
1. Verify customer has signed contract
2. If not printed, go to Reservation Approval page
3. Find reservation in "Downpayment Paid" or "Approved" tab
4. Print contract
5. Have customer sign both copies
6. Give one copy to customer, keep one for records

### At Checkout
1. Open Reservation Approval page
2. Find customer's reservation
3. Check remaining balance
4. Collect payment
5. Print final receipt (same as contract)
6. Mark as completed in system

## Tips & Best Practices

### For Managers:
- ✅ Review all details before approval
- ✅ Set reasonable downpayment amounts (30% is standard)
- ✅ Always provide rejection reasons for record-keeping
- ✅ Print contracts immediately after approval
- ✅ Keep signed contracts in customer files

### For Front Desk:
- ✅ Always verify customer ID matches reservation
- ✅ Confirm payment amounts match the contract
- ✅ Keep signed contracts secure
- ✅ Give customer their copy of the contract
- ✅ Verify remaining balance before checkout

## Troubleshooting

### "Manager credentials required"
- Ensure you're logged in as Manager or Admin
- Double-check email and password are correct
- Contact IT if credentials don't work

### Reservation not showing
- Check you're on the correct tab
- Try "All" tab to see all statuses
- Refresh the page
- Contact IT if still missing

### Print not working
- Check browser print settings
- Try a different browser
- Ensure printer is connected
- Use "Print Contract" button, not browser print

### Downpayment amount incorrect
- Verify calculation: Downpayment + Balance = Total
- If wrong, reject and request customer to resubmit
- Contact manager to override if needed

## Quick Reference: Payment Options

| Payment Option | When to Use | Customer Pays | Balance at Checkout |
|---------------|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Pay Upon Checkout | Trust customer, no deposit needed | Nothing upfront | Full amount |
| Require Downpayment | Secure booking, especially for events | 30-50% upfront | Remaining balance |
