# Housekeeping Staff Quick Guide

## How to Access

1. Log in to the system
2. Click on **"Housekeeping"** in the left menu
3. Or click on **"Reservation"** → **"🧹 Housekeeping"**

---

## Understanding the Dashboard

### Stats at the Top (The Numbers)
- **Yellow Box (🔔)** - Rooms waiting to be cleaned
- **Blue Box (🧹)** - Rooms you're currently cleaning
- **Green Box (✅)** - Rooms you've finished cleaning
- **Gray Box (🏨)** - Total rooms in hotel

---

## How to Update Room Status

### When You Start Cleaning a Room:
1. Find the room in the "Needs Cleaning" section (yellow)
2. Click the **"🧹 Start"** button
3. The room moves to "In Progress" (blue)

### When You Finish Cleaning a Room:
1. Find the room in the "In Progress" section (blue)
2. Click the **"✅ Complete"** button
3. The room moves to "Completed" (green)
4. Room automatically becomes "Available" for guests

### If a Room Needs Re-cleaning:
1. Click the **"🔄 Reset"** button on the completed room
2. Room goes back to "Needs Cleaning"

---

## How to Add Notes

### Why Add Notes?
- Report broken items
- Note missing supplies
- Record special requests
- Document issues found

### Steps:
1. Find the room in the list
2. Click **"✏️ Edit"** button
3. Type your notes in the text box
   - Example: "Replaced broken light bulb"
   - Example: "Need more towels"
   - Example: "Carpet stain in corner"
4. Click **"Update Status"**

---

## Using Filters (Finding Rooms Faster)

### Filter Buttons:
- **All Rooms** - Shows everything
- **🔔 Pending** - Shows only rooms to clean (MOST USEFUL!)
- **🧹 In Progress** - Shows what you're working on
- **✅ Completed** - Shows finished rooms

### Search Box:
Type to find specific:
- Room number (e.g., "101")
- Room type (e.g., "Deluxe")

---

## Daily Workflow

### Morning Start:
1. Click "🔔 Pending" to see all rooms to clean
2. Note the yellow number - that's your workload
3. Start with checkouts first (usually marked in system)

### During Work:
1. When entering room: Click "🧹 Start"
2. Clean the room
3. When done: Click "✅ Complete"
4. Add notes if needed
5. Move to next room

### Before Leaving:
1. Check if all "In Progress" rooms are completed
2. Add any final notes
3. Yellow number should be 0 or low

---

## Status Meanings

### 🔔 Pending (Yellow)
- **What it means:** Room needs cleaning
- **What to do:** Click "Start" when you begin

### 🧹 In Progress (Blue)
- **What it means:** Room is being cleaned right now
- **What to do:** Click "Complete" when done

### ✅ Completed (Green)
- **What it means:** Room is clean and ready for guests
- **What to do:** Nothing, unless it needs re-cleaning

### 🔍 Inspected (Purple)
- **What it means:** Supervisor checked and approved
- **What to do:** Nothing, supervisor handles this

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Guest Just Checked Out
**What you see:** Room appears in Pending (yellow) list
**What to do:**
1. Click "🧹 Start"
2. Clean the room
3. Click "✅ Complete"
4. Front desk gets notified it's ready

### Scenario 2: Found Damaged Item
**What you see:** Room needs note about damage
**What to do:**
1. Click "✏️ Edit"
2. Write note: "Broken TV remote, needs replacement"
3. Click "Update Status"
4. Maintenance will be notified

### Scenario 3: Need More Supplies
**What you see:** Running low on supplies
**What to do:**
1. Click "✏️ Edit" on any room
2. Write note: "Need more shampoo bottles"
3. Manager sees the note
4. Supplies will be restocked

### Scenario 4: Room Needs Re-clean
**What you see:** Supervisor says room needs more work
**What to do:**
1. Supervisor clicks "🔄 Reset"
2. Room appears in your Pending list again
3. Re-clean the room
4. Click "Complete" when done

## Tips for Success

### ✅ DO:
- Click "Start" when entering room
- Click "Complete" immediately when done
- Add notes for any issues
- Check the dashboard regularly
- Ask supervisor if unsure

### ❌ DON'T:
- Forget to mark rooms complete
- Leave rooms in "In Progress" overnight
- Skip adding notes for problems

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Can't see the Housekeeping page
**Fix:** Ask your supervisor to give you "Housekeeping" role access

### Problem: Buttons don't work
**Fix:** 
1. Refresh the page (F5)
2. Check internet connection
3. Call IT if still not working

### Problem: Room status won't change
**Fix:**
1. Wait 3 seconds and try again
2. Check if you clicked the right button
3. Ask supervisor for help

### Problem: Lost track of what you're cleaning
**Fix:** Click "🧹 In Progress" filter to see current rooms
